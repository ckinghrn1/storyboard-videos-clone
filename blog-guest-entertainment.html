Fun and Games: Entertaining Your Guests

Making memories for everyone

A wedding is a celebration, and a huge part of that is ensuring your guests have a fantastic time. Beyond the ceremony and the meal, providing entertainment is a great way to keep the energy up and create wonderful candid moments for your wedding film. Here are some of our favorite entertainment ideas.

Lawn Games

If you're having an outdoor reception, lawn games are an absolute winner. From giant Jenga and cornhole to croquet and petanque, these games give guests of all ages something fun to do. We love capturing the competitive spirits and the laughter that these games always inspire.

Live Music

Music sets the tone for the entire day. While a great DJ is essential for the evening, consider live music for your drinks reception. A roaming band, a solo acoustic guitarist, or even a saxophonist can add a sophisticated and lively atmosphere that sounds as good as it looks on film.

Interactive Food Stations

Why not make the food part of the entertainment? Pizza trucks, ice cream carts, or even a build-your-own taco bar are always a hit. They provide a focal point for guests to gather and lead to some great candid shots of people enjoying themselves.

Photobooths and Video Guestbooks

Photobooths are a classic for a reason—they're just plain fun! A video guestbook is a great alternative (or addition) that allows guests to leave you heartfelt messages on camera. These are often some of the most cherished parts of a wedding film.

Magicians and Caricaturists

For something a bit different, roaming entertainers like magicians or caricaturists can provide unexpected moments of joy and surprise. Their interactions with guests are perfect for capturing genuine reactions and laughter.

Think About the Kids

If you have children attending, providing a dedicated space or some specific entertainment for them is a lifesaver. Bubbles, activity packs, or even a children's entertainer can keep them happy and engaged, allowing the adults to relax and enjoy the celebration.
