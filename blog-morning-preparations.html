Wedding Morning Preparations: A Survival Guide

Keeping it calm before the "I do"

The morning of your wedding is the start of one of the biggest days of your life. It can also be one of the most chaotic! As videographers, we're with you from the very beginning, and we've seen it all. Here's how to ensure your morning is relaxed, fun, and beautifully captured on film.

Choose a Bright Space

Light is everything in video. If possible, pick a room with large windows and plenty of natural light for your preparations. Not only does it help your makeup artist, but it also makes for much more flattering and cinematic footage. Clear away any clutter near the windows to give us space to work.

Create a "Details" Corner

Have your dress, shoes, jewelry, and stationery all in one place before we arrive. This allows us to start capturing those beautiful detail shots immediately without having to hunt for things. It's a great way to set the scene and add context to your film.

Think About Your Outfit

What you wear while getting ready will be on camera! Consider matching robes or comfortable, stylish pajamas for you and your bridesmaids. It adds a cohesive and polished look to the morning shots and makes everyone feel a bit more special.

Keep the Music Going

A good playlist can transform the mood of the room. Whether you want something upbeat to get the energy going or something calm to keep the nerves at bay, music adds a wonderful layer of emotion to the morning prep. We love capturing people singing along and laughing together.

Don't Forget to Eat!

It sounds simple, but it's easy to forget to eat when you're caught up in the excitement. Have some light snacks and plenty of water (and maybe a bit of bubbly!) on hand. A well-fed bridal party is a happy bridal party, and that joy will shine through on camera.

Be Present

Finally, try to stay in the moment. Don't worry about the logistics—that's what your toasted or coordinator is for. Enjoy the time with your closest friends and family. Those genuine smiles and heartfelt moments are exactly what we're there to capture.
