We're Stuart and the Storyboard team — a group of passionate creatives who believe every wedding deserves to be remembered, not just photographed. We make fun, heartfelt wedding films that bring back all the laughs, the tears, and the dance-floor moments you forgot happened.

Based in Essex, we cover weddings across London, Surrey, Sussex, Hampshire, Kent and the South of England.

How we film

Our style is discreet and unobtrusive. We blend into your day so you can be totally yourself. No awkward posed shots, no "look at the camera" moments — just you, your people, and the joy of the moment.

We use a mix of cinematic and documentary techniques to create a film that feels natural, emotional and true to your day.
