Say I Do to Stress-Free Planning

Tips and tricks for a relaxed wedding day

Wedding planning should be exciting, not stressful! We've filmed hundreds of weddings, and we've picked up a few things along the way. Here are our top tips for keeping things relaxed and making sure you actually enjoy the process.

Start with the big stuff

Venue, date, and guest list — nail these three things early and everything else falls into place. Don't get bogged down in napkin colours before you've sorted the fundamentals.

Build a realistic budget

Be honest about what you can afford and prioritise the things that matter most to you. If food and drink is your thing, splash out there. If entertainment is what gets you excited, put your money into a killer band. There's no right or wrong way to allocate your budget.

Delegate, delegate, delegate

You don't have to do everything yourself. Your bridal party, family, and wedding planner (if you have one) are all there to help. Let them! Assign specific tasks to specific people and trust them to get it done.

Don't sweat the small stuff

Here's a secret: your guests won't notice if the ribbons on the chairs don't perfectly match the flowers. They're there to celebrate with you, not to judge your table decorations. Focus on the experience, not the details.

Plan some downtime

Build in some breathing room on the day itself. Whether it's 20 minutes alone with your partner after the ceremony, or a quiet drink before the reception kicks off, those moments of calm make all the difference.

Remember why you're doing this

At the end of the day (literally), you're marrying the person you love. That's it. Everything else is just a bonus. If something goes wrong, laugh it off. Some of the best moments we've ever captured on film are the ones that weren't planned.

  • Book your key suppliers early — the best ones get snapped up fast
  • Create a shared spreadsheet — keep everything in one place
  • Take breaks from planning — wedding-free weekends are a must
  • Say no to things you don't want — it's your day, not anyone else's
  • Trust your suppliers — they've done this hundreds of times
