Why We Love Kew Gardens Weddings

A botanical paradise for your big day

Kew Gardens is more than just a botanical garden; it's a world-class UNESCO World Heritage site and one of the most breathtaking wedding venues on the planet. As wedding videographers, filming at Kew is always a highlight of our calendar. Here's why we think it's so special.

Iconic Glasshouses

The Palm House and the Temperate House are architectural masterpieces. They provide a lush, tropical backdrop that is completely unique. The way the light filters through the glass and the exotic plants creates a dreamlike quality in every shot. It's like being in a different world without ever leaving London.

Endless Variety

With over 300 acres of gardens, the variety of locations within Kew is staggering. From the classic elegance of Cambridge Cottage to the grand symmetry of the Broadwalk, there's a perfect spot for every part of your day. We love taking couples for a wander through the gardens to find those hidden, quiet pockets that feel completely secluded.

The Great Broadwalk Borders

The Broadwalk is one of the most stunning promenades in London. Lined with vibrant flowers and framing the Hive in the distance, it's a fantastic location for movement shots. We love capturing couples strolling along here as the sun starts to set.

A Sense of History

Kew has been a center of botanical excellence for centuries, and you can feel that history in every corner. It adds a layer of significance and grandeur to your wedding day that is truly unique. It's a venue that will leave a lasting impression on you and your guests.

Cinematic Perfection

Everything about Kew is designed to be beautiful. From the meticulously kept gardens to the grand historic buildings, it's a place where every angle is a winner. For a wedding film, it provides a level of cinematic scale that is hard to match anywhere else.
